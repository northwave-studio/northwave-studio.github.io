Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Northwave Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile apps we publish, including CKA Exam Prep (together, the "Apps").

In short: our Apps don't require an account, and your study progress stays on your device. We use Google AdMob to show ads to free users and RevenueCat with Google Play to handle in-app purchases. These services may collect some device information, as described below. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your quiz answers, progress, bookmarks and settings (such as light or dark theme) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. You can erase them at any time with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the App.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and we do not run our own servers that receive data from the Apps. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free versions of the Apps show ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (for example, device model, operating system and app version),
  • interactions with ads (for example, views and taps).

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later from the App's settings. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users who purchase Pro do not see ads.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have purchased Pro and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives purchase information (such as the product purchased, transaction details and an anonymous app user ID) from the App and Google Play.

Learn more: RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above, so they can provide ads and process purchases, or where required by law.

6. Children

The Apps are designed for adults and students preparing for professional exams and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you reset your progress or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google AdMob and RevenueCat is kept according to their own policies. To ask about deleting data connected to your use of the Apps, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

We rely on the security of your device and of our service providers. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit what is collected to what the Apps need to work.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new App or service. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
